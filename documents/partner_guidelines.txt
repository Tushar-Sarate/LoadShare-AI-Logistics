LoadShare Food - Delivery Partner Guidelines

Registration
A delivery partner must register with the role delivery_partner and create a profile with phone number, vehicle type, vehicle number, city and pincode.
Each phone number and each vehicle number can be used for only one partner account.

Earnings
A partner earns the delivery fee of every delivered order. The fee depends on the trip distance: Rs 20 for the first 2 km and Rs 7 for every extra km on food orders.
Earnings, distance covered and delivery history are shown on the partner dashboard.

Availability
A partner is marked unavailable when an order is assigned and becomes available again when the order is Delivered or Cancelled.
A partner can have only one active order at a time.
The nearest available partner is assigned first.

Delivery Process
1. Collect the order from the restaurant and press Picked up from restaurant.
2. Press Out for delivery while travelling to the customer.
3. For Cash on Delivery orders, collect the exact amount and press Cash collected.
4. Hand over the order and press Mark delivered.
Statuses must be updated in this order. A status cannot be skipped.
A Cash on Delivery order cannot be marked delivered until the cash is confirmed as collected.

Conduct
Partners must be polite and carry a valid vehicle document.
Partners must not open or damage the order.
Partners must not ask the customer for extra money beyond the amount shown in the app.

Issues
If the customer is not reachable or the address is wrong, contact support before cancelling.
Repeated complaints can lead to the partner account being deactivated by the admin.
