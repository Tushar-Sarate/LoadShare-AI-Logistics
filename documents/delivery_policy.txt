LoadShare Food - Delivery Policy

Delivery Charges for Food Orders
The delivery fee depends on the distance between the restaurant and the delivery address.
The first 2 km cost a flat Rs 20.
After 2 km, Rs 7 is added for every extra km.
Example: a 1 km trip costs Rs 20. A 5 km trip costs Rs 41. A 10 km trip costs Rs 76.
The delivery fee is shown in the cart before the order is placed. The food price is charged separately.
The maximum delivery distance for food orders is 25 km.

Delivery Charges for Parcels
The parcel delivery charge is Rs 30 for the first 2 km, plus Rs 9 for every extra km, plus Rs 5 per kg of package weight.
Example: a 2 kg parcel travelling 12 km costs Rs 30 + Rs 90 + Rs 10 = Rs 130.
Maximum parcel weight is 50 kg.

Estimated Delivery Time
Every order shows an estimated arrival time in minutes. It depends on the distance, the number of items and the time of day.

Order Status Flow
An order moves through these statuses in order: Pending, Assigned, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered.
An order can also be Cancelled.
Pending: the order is placed and waiting for a delivery partner.
Assigned: the nearest available delivery partner has been assigned.
Picked Up: the partner has collected the order from the restaurant.
In Transit: the order is on the way to the delivery address.
Delivered: the order has reached the customer.

Partner Assignment
Orders are assigned to the nearest available delivery partner within 20 km of the pickup point.
If a partner is sharing live GPS, the real position is used. Otherwise the position is estimated from the partner's pincode.
If no partner is available nearby, the order stays Pending until one becomes free.

Live Tracking
While an order is on the way, the customer can open Live tracking to see the delivery partner on a map, with the distance left to the delivery address.
The location is shared only while the order is Assigned, Picked Up or In Transit.

Ratings and Reviews
After an order is delivered, the customer can rate the restaurant from 1 to 5 stars and write a short review. The average rating is shown on the restaurant card.

Delays Due to Weather
During heavy rain, floods or storms, deliveries may take longer than the estimated time.
Delivery partners are asked to deliver safely, and a delay caused by weather is not a reason for cancellation after the order is picked up.
If an order is very late, the customer can raise a complaint and the support team will review it.

Package Rules
Prohibited parcel items: illegal goods, explosives, flammable liquids and perishable items without proper packing.
Pickup and delivery addresses must be complete and include a 6-digit pincode.

Tracking
Customers can view the full status history of an order, with the time of each change, and see the pickup and drop locations on a map.
