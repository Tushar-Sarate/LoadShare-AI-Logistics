LoadShare Food - Refund Policy

Cancellation
A customer can cancel an order only while its status is Pending.
After a partner is assigned, the customer must raise a complaint and the admin can cancel the order.
Delivered orders cannot be cancelled.

Refund Rules
If a prepaid order (UPI) is cancelled while Pending, the full amount is refunded, including the delivery fee.
Cash on Delivery (COD) orders have no refund because no payment is taken before delivery.
If an order is lost or damaged by the delivery partner, the amount is refunded after the complaint is verified.
Refunds are approved by the support team. The AI assistant cannot approve a refund.

Refund Time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Payment Methods
Accepted methods are UPI and Cash on Delivery.
A UPI payment is marked Paid when the customer enters the UPI reference number after paying through the QR code.
For Cash on Delivery, the customer pays cash to the delivery partner. The order is marked Paid when the partner confirms the cash was collected.

Complaints
Customers can raise a complaint for a late, lost, damaged or wrongly delivered order, in the app or by email.
Complaint statuses are Open, In Progress and Resolved.
A request to cancel a Pending order is done automatically. Other complaints are reviewed by the support team, who reply within 24 hours.
